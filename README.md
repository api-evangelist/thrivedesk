ThriveDesk provides an AI‑powered customer service and chatbot platform for e‑commerce businesses. It offers a unified inbox for email, chat, phone, SMS and WhatsApp, along with automation, analytics, and AI tools such as skills, guidance, training, escalation and memory. The platform includes a modern MCP server for AI assistants to search conversations and draft responses, and integrates with popular e‑commerce tools. Pricing plans cater to small teams and enterprises, and extensive documentation and support resources are available.

**Areas:** Customer Service · AI · Chatbot · E-commerce · Help Desk

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for ThriveDesk: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
